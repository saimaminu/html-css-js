Who We Are

The IT Aid is a leading IT training and skills development institute based in Sylhet, Bangladesh. We are committed to building a digitally empowered generation by delivering high-quality, practical, and career-focused technology education. Our programs are designed for students, fresh graduates, working professionals, and entrepreneurs who want to thrive in the digital economy. With a faculty of experienced industry professionals, state-of-the-art training facilities, and a proven curriculum, The IT Aid stands as a center of excellence for IT education in the Sylhet region.
